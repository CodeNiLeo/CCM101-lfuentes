# Mission Reflection

## Reflection

During this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. I was particularly impressed by its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. Its services such as Vertex AI, BigQuery, and Google Kubernetes Engine provide useful tools for organizations developing modern and data-driven applications.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services that allow organizations to use computing resources over the internet. All three platforms offer virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity and access management, Kubernetes, and other services. They also provide scalable infrastructure, security features, global infrastructure, and pay-as-you-go pricing models.

Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection because every organization has different goals, technologies, budgets, and workloads. For example, a company that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its strong Microsoft integration. On the other hand, an AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities, while a global organization may choose AWS because of its broad range of services and global infrastructure.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that recommendations should be based on the client's specific requirements, including budget, scalability, existing technologies, performance, security, and future growth.

My **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** has also improved significantly after completing this mission. I learned how to organize research, comparison tables, recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and reflections using Markdown. The portfolio now demonstrates not only my knowledge of cloud platforms but also my ability to analyze business requirements and recommend appropriate cloud solutions.
